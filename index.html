How Lead Pomelo works differently

We count conversations, not emails sent

Problem

Our last provider sent thousands of emails a month and reported open rates. Most replies came from brokers, and a few owners were annoyed we had written at all. None of it turned into a real conversation about their business.

Search fund principal

Industrial services thesis

Solution

We report one number: owners who agreed to talk to you. Each one is checked against your buy box before we pass it on, and you see the reply thread in full. If an owner doesn’t fit your criteria, they don’t count.

We never write to one owner for two buyers
Once an owner is on your list, they stay yours. We check every new list against every active search, so an owner never hears from two of our clients.
Every message goes out in your name
Emails are sent from your domain and signed by you, in your voice. Lead Pomelo never appears in them, and owners deal with you from the first line.
Lists built by software, checked by people
Automated tools find companies and filings; a researcher confirms the owner, the address and the fit before a single note goes out.
First notes out within three weeks
Week one we agree the buy box, week two we build and check the list, week three the first notes go out from your inbox.
Data and sending costs included
Registry data, contact lookups and sending inboxes are part of the monthly fee. You won’t get a separate invoice for any of them.
A monthly report you could send to investors
Companies mapped, owners contacted, replies and conversations, each with its source and date. We review it with you every month and narrow the buy box when the numbers say so.
